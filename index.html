About TerraTrio

An interactive exploration app designed to analyze many features that help you find analogies across Earth, Mars and Moon.

Click and drag to spin the globe. Use your mouse scroll to zoom in and out.

Use the top menu buttons to toggle between Earth, Mars and Moon or pause the rotation.

Double-click anywhere on the planet's surface or use the right sidebar to pin custom coordinates.

View Location info by single clicking any active red marker on the map to open its info card.

Choose different features from the left sidebar to active a layer that shows locations where a given parameter occurs
