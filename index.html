About TerraTrio

TerraTrio is an interactive 3D planetary exploration app designed to analyze geographic coordinates, geological formations, and planetary analogues across Earth, Mars, and the Moon.

Quick Guide

  • Rotate & Zoom: Click and drag to spin the globe. Use your mouse scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Switch Bodies: Use the top menu buttons to toggle between Earth, Mars, and the Moon, or pause the rotation.
  • Add Custom Points: Double-click anywhere on the planet's surface or use the right sidebar form to pin custom coordinates.
  • View Location Info: Single-click any active red marker on the map to open its info card and access detailed scientific logs.
